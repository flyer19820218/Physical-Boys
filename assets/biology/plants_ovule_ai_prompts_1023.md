# 第五課「胚珠」補充：Gemini 插圖咒語（2026-10-23）

## 怎麼用

- 一段咒語生一張圖，整段（含最後的 Style）一起貼給 Gemini。生好貼回對話，告訴我編號（例如「OV1」）。
- 畫面裡**不能有任何字、箭頭、標籤、浮水印**。有的話請它重生。
- 底色都是**白底**（近白即可）。
- **圖裡不要畫花粉管、精細胞、細胞核**：花粉管、胚囊裡的 8 個核、受精動作我用程式畫（AI 容易數錯核的數量，這一課偏偏最怕數錯）。圖只負責「外形與構造的質感」。
- 這些咒語從科學內容重新描述，**不是**重畫課本圖，不要把課本圖丟給 Gemini 當參考。
- 必要：OV1、OV2。可選：OV3。

## 我拿圖來做什麼

| 編號 | 畫面 | 用途 |
|---|---|---|
| OV1 | 單一倒生胚珠縱切（珠柄、珠孔、兩層珠被、珠心、空的胚囊） | 「單胚珠」：放大看胚囊，程式疊上 8 個核（3 反足、2 極核、2 助細胞、1 卵）和花粉管進珠孔 |
| OV2 | 多胚珠子房縱切（胚珠成兩排接在胎座上、上方連花柱） | 「多胚珠」：每顆胚珠各接一條花粉管，數花粉管、算精細胞，胚珠→種子 |
| OV3（可選） | 剖開的豌豆莢，豆子用細柄接在莢的一側 | 生活中的多胚珠例子：一個莢裡幾顆豆，就是幾顆受精的胚珠 |

## OV1　單一倒生胚珠縱切

```
A single anatropous ovule of a flowering plant, shown as a clean longitudinal cross-section, large and centered on a plain white background.

The ovule is egg-shaped and bent over (anatropous): a short stalk (funiculus) comes in from the lower right and curves around, so the small opening at the tip of the ovule (the micropyle) points downward, close to the stalk, at the bottom center. The ovule has two clearly visible outer layers (integuments), pale green and cream, that wrap an inner tissue (nucellus), and in the middle of the nucellus there is one large, smooth, empty, pale-yellow oval cavity (the embryo sac). The embryo sac is EMPTY: no cells, no nuclei, no dots inside it. Where the integuments leave a small gap at the bottom, the micropyle is a narrow open channel leading from the outside to the embryo sac. At the opposite end (top) is the chalaza, where the layers join and the stalk's vascular strand ends.

Only one ovule, nothing else in the picture: no ovary wall, no style, no pollen, no pollen tube, no sperm, no nuclei, no text, no labels, no arrows.

Style: soft botanical teaching illustration, watercolor and fine ink line, muted greens and creams, gentle shading, clear edges, high detail on the cell layers, flat white background, no shadow on the background, 16:9.
```

**檢查：** 只有一顆胚珠；珠孔朝下靠近珠柄；胚囊是空的（沒有細胞、核、點）；沒有花粉管、沒有字。

## OV2　多胚珠子房縱切

```
A longitudinal cross-section of the ovary of a flowering plant that contains MANY ovules, large and centered on a plain white background, with the long axis vertical.

The ovary is a pale green, elongated chamber. A narrow style (a slim hollow column) rises from the top center and ends in a small broad stigma at the very top. Inside the ovary, five ovules hang on short stalks from a thick ridge of tissue (the placenta) running down the left inner wall of the ovary; they are stacked one under another in a single vertical row, evenly spaced, all about the same size, each ovule egg-shaped and cream-colored with a visible outer layer. A sixth and seventh ovule are hidden, so do not draw more than five. Between the ovules there is empty space inside the ovary chamber. The ovary wall is thick, pale green, with fine texture.

Nothing else in the picture: no pollen grains, no pollen tubes, no sperm, no cell nuclei inside the ovules, no text, no labels, no arrows, no leaves, no flower petals.

Style: soft botanical teaching illustration, watercolor and fine ink line, muted greens and creams, gentle shading, clear edges, flat white background, no shadow on the background, 16:9.
```

**檢查：** 剛好 5 顆胚珠、排成一直排接在同一側的胎座上；花柱＋柱頭在頂端；沒有花粉、花粉管、字。

```
An opened garden pea pod lying flat on a plain white background, seen from the side, its two halves spread open like a book, large and centered.

Inside the open pod, exactly six round green peas sit in one row along the pod's seam, each pea attached to the pod by a tiny short stalk. The pod is bright green with a smooth glossy outer surface and a paler, slightly furry inner surface. The six peas are all similar in size and shape.

Nothing else: no leaves, no flowers, no hands, no text, no labels, no arrows.

Style: soft botanical teaching illustration, watercolor and fine ink line, natural greens, gentle shading, clear edges, flat white background, no shadow on the background, 16:9.
```

**檢查：** 剛好 6 顆豆、排成一排、各有細柄接在莢上；沒有字。
